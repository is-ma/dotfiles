# GNU Stow

Generar enlaces simbólicos (-t: target):

`stow -t $HOME/.local/share/fonts font_files/`

... o incluso tener convertir a dotfiles en destino:

`stow -t $HOME/ --dotfiles bash_files/`

Restow (re-aplicar enlaces):

`stow -R -t $HOME/.local/share/fonts font_files/`

Delete (eliminar enlaces):

`stow -D -t $HOME/.local/share/fonts font_files/`
